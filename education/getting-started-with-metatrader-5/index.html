Getting started with MetaTrader 5

A quick tour of MetaTrader 5: logging in, adding instruments to Market Watch, opening a chart and placing your first order on desktop or mobile.

Varexa Markets Education 2 Nov 2026 6 min readBeginner

MetaTrader 5 (MT5) is the trading platform used for Varexa Markets accounts. It is available for Windows, macOS, web browsers, iOS and Android. This guide covers the first things you will do after installing it.

1. Log in to your account

After your account is opened, you receive a login number, password and server name. In MT5, open File â€º Login to Trade Account on desktop, or Settings â€º New account on mobile, search for the server and enter your details. [Server name to be supplied by Varexa Markets.]

2. Set up Market Watch

The Market Watch window lists instruments with their live bid and ask prices. Right-click and choose Symbols to show or hide instruments, such as EUR/USD, XAU/USD or US 500.

3. Open and customise a chart

Drag a symbol from Market Watch onto the chart area. Use the toolbar to switch between candlestick, bar and line charts and to choose a timeframe. Add indicators from the Insert â€º Indicators menu.

4. Place an order

Press New Order (or F9 on desktop). Choose the volume in lots, then add a stop loss and take profit if you want them. Select Market Execution to trade at the current price, or a pending order type to trade at a level you choose.

5. Monitor your trades

Open positions appear in the Toolbox â€º Trade tab on desktop and the Trade tab on mobile, with live profit or loss. Closed trades appear in History.

Key takeaways

  • Use the same MT5 login on desktop, web and mobile.
  • Market Watch controls which instruments you see.
  • Add a stop loss when you place an order, not afterwards.

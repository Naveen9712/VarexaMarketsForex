Go long or short

A CFD lets you take a position on a price rising (buy) or falling (sell) without owning the underlying asset.

Leverage and margin

Margin is the deposit needed to open a leveraged position. Leverage magnifies losses just as much as gains.

Spreads and costs

The spread is the gap between the buy and sell price. Some accounts also charge commission, and positions held overnight may incur swap.

Pips and lot sizes

A pip is a small standard unit of price movement. Your lot size decides how much each pip is worth to you.

Market hours

Forex trades 24 hours a day, five days a week. Metals, energies, indices and shares follow their own sessions.

Risk management tools

Stop-loss and take-profit orders close a position at levels you set. A standard stop-loss can fill at a worse price in fast markets.

Leveraged products carry a high level of risk and may not be suitable for everyone. Make sure you understand how they work before you trade.

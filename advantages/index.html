Trading conditions

Know your costs before you trade

Every trade has a cost, and leverage changes how much a price move affects your account. These are the conditions Varexa Markets will publish for each account type.

Spread
The difference between the buy (ask) and sell (bid) price. It is the main cost on many accounts.
Commission
A fixed charge per lot traded, applied on some account types in exchange for tighter spreads.
Leverage
Lets you open a larger position with a smaller deposit (margin). It increases losses as well as gains.
Swap
An overnight financing charge or credit on positions held past the daily rollover.
Execution
How orders are filled, including order types and what happens in fast-moving markets.

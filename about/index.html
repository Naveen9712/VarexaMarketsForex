Company overview

Access to global markets

Varexa Markets is a forex and CFD brokerage offering access to currency pairs and CFDs on precious metals, energies, global indices and shares. Clients can trade through MetaTrader 5 on desktop, web and mobile.

Before opening an account, review our product specifications, trading costs, funding and withdrawal procedures, and the risks of leveraged trading. Our Client Agreement identifies the company you contract with and sets out the terms that apply to your account.

Varexa Markets Ltd is incorporated in Saint Lucia. Product and account availability depends on your country of residence and eligibility.

Mission and vision

What we work toward

Our mission

Make global markets easier to navigate. We aim to give traders straightforward access to global markets and the information they need before placing a trade, including clear information about costs, margin requirements and risks, intuitive trading tools, and accessible support.

Our vision

Earn lasting trust as an international broker. We aim to make our terms easy to verify, communicate changes clearly and deliver consistent service from account opening through to withdrawal.

Our approach

Know what to expect before you open an account

You should be able to compare account costs, understand how orders are handled and see how deposits and withdrawals work. Our Trading Conditions and Legal Documents set out those details so you can review the terms before deciding whether to trade.

We focus on clear pricing information, a consistent MetaTrader 5 experience, practical market education, and accessible support for account, platform and payment questions.

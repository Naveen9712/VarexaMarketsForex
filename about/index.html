Our mission

Make global markets easier to navigate

Our mission is to give traders straightforward access to global markets and the information they need before placing a trade. That means making costs, margin requirements and risks easy to understand, providing intuitive trading tools, and making it easy to reach support when questions arise.

Our vision

Earn lasting trust as an international broker

Our vision is for Varexa Markets to become a broker that traders and partners choose for the long term. As we grow, we aim to earn that trust by making our terms easy to verify, communicating changes clearly, and delivering consistent service from account opening through to withdrawal.

Our objectives

What we focus on at Varexa Markets

  1. Make spreads, commissions and margin requirements easy to compare before choosing an account.

  2. Provide a consistent MetaTrader 5 experience across desktop, web and mobile.

  3. Explain leverage, margin and stop-out levels in plain language before clients trade.

  4. Help traders build their market knowledge through practical guides, videos and market analysis.

  5. Make deposit and withdrawal methods, fees and processing times easy to find.

  6. Make it easy to reach support for account, platform and payment questions.

Our approach

Our approach to brokerage

Company overview

Who we are

Varexa Markets is a forex and CFD brokerage. Clients trade currency pairs, gold and silver, energies, global indices and stock CFDs from a single account on MetaTrader 5, available on desktop, web and mobile.

Alongside self-directed trading, Varexa Markets offers copy trading, PAMM accounts, funded trading programmes and partner programmes, supported by educational content and market updates.

Our mission

Make global markets accessible, clear and fair

To give traders straightforward access to the world's financial markets, with clear information about costs and risks, dependable technology and support from people who understand trading.

Our vision

A trusted international broker for every kind of trader

To be recognised as a transparent, client-focused broker that serves beginners, experienced traders, money managers and partners alike, and grows by earning their long-term trust.

Objectives

What we are working towards

  • Publish clear, up-to-date trading conditions for every account type
  • Provide reliable access to MetaTrader 5 on every device
  • Help clients learn through videos, guides and market analysis
  • Offer services that suit different goals and levels of experience
  • Respond to clients quickly through chat, email and phone
  • Explain the risks of leveraged trading in plain language
Company values

The standards we hold ourselves to

The principles behind how Varexa Markets serves clients and partners.

Transparency

Costs, conditions and risks are explained clearly before clients trade, with policies published and easy to find.

Client first

Decisions start with what helps clients trade with confidence, from account opening to everyday support.

Integrity

Fair treatment for every client and partner, with honest communication and no hidden terms.

Responsible trading

Leveraged products carry high risk. We encourage clients to understand them, practise first and manage risk carefully.

Service excellence

Knowledgeable, responsive support through the channels clients prefer, during market hours.

Continuous improvement

We keep refining our platform access, services and education based on client feedback and market change.

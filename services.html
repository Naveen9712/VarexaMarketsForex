01

Social and copy trading

Copy trading lets you follow strategy providers and automatically replicate their trades in your own account, in proportion to the amount you allocate. You stay in control: choose who to follow, set your own limits and stop at any time.

  • Beginners
  • Hands-off traders
Start copy trading

How it works

  1. Browse providersReview strategy providers, their trading history, risk level and fees.
  2. Set your allocationChoose how much to allocate and set limits such as a maximum loss.
  3. Trades are copiedWhen the provider opens or closes a trade, it is mirrored in your account in proportion to your allocation.
  4. Monitor or stopTrack every copied trade from your account and pause or stop copying whenever you choose.

Features and benefits

  • Learn by watching how experienced traders manage positions
  • Follow more than one provider to spread your exposure
  • Keep full ownership of your account and funds
  • Set your own risk limits per provider

Risks

Copying a strategy does not guarantee the same results. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Copied trades can lose money, and differences in timing, price or account size mean your results can differ from the provider's.

02

PAMM accounts

A PAMM (Percentage Allocation Management Module) account lets investors place funds with a money manager, who trades the combined capital through one master account. Profits and losses are shared between investors in proportion to each one's share of the pool.

  • Investors
  • Money managers
Explore PAMM

How it works

  1. Choose a managerReview each money manager's strategy, track record, fees and terms.
  2. Invest in the poolYour funds join the manager's PAMM account alongside other investors' funds.
  3. Manager tradesThe manager trades the pooled capital. Investors do not place trades themselves.
  4. Results are allocatedProfits and losses are distributed by each investor's percentage share, after the manager's fees.

Features and benefits

  • Access a professional manager's strategy without trading yourself
  • Results allocated in proportion to your share
  • Regular statements on performance and fees
  • Managers can trade larger capital from one account

Risks

Investors share in the manager's losses as well as profits, and can lose some or all of the amount invested. The manager makes all trading decisions. Withdrawals may be limited to set dates under the PAMM agreement.

Funded accounts and prop trading

A funded trading programme lets you prove your strategy in an evaluation. If you meet the targets within the risk rules, you trade an account funded with firm capital and keep a share of the profits you make.

  • Experienced traders
  • Evaluation
Get funded

How it works

  1. Choose a programmeSelect an account size and register for the evaluation.
  2. Pass the evaluationReach the profit target without breaking the daily or overall loss limits.
  3. Trade firm capitalOnce you qualify, you trade a funded account under the same risk rules.
  4. Share the profitsProfits are split between you and the firm on the agreed schedule.

Applicable conditions and risks

Understand the risks first

Every service involves leveraged products that can lose money. Read these points and the full terms before taking part.

Leverage and losses

Forex and CFDs are leveraged. Small price moves can cause large losses, and you can lose your capital.

Past performance

A provider's, manager's or trader's past results are not a reliable indicator of future results.

Third-party decisions

With copy trading and PAMM, someone else makes the trading decisions that affect your funds.

Market conditions

Volatility, gaps and low liquidity can cause slippage, so trades may fill at different prices than expected.

Programme rules

Each service has its own agreement, fees and eligibility rules, which apply in addition to the client agreement.

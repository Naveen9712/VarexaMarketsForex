Identity and ownership checks

Before activating an account, we request information needed to verify identity and assess eligibility. This may include government-issued identification, proof of address and details about the intended use of the account. For organisations, we may also verify registration, authorised representatives and beneficial owners. We may request additional information, including source of funds or wealth, where appropriate.

Screening and ongoing monitoring

We assess account and transaction activity for unusual patterns and may refresh information when details change or checks become due. We apply risk-based screening for applicable sanctions and politically exposed persons. A request for additional information is part of the verification process and does not by itself indicate wrongdoing.

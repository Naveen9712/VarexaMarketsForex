Parties and scope

This agreement is between [contracting entity, registration number, registered address and verified regulatory status] (we, us) and the individual or organisation identified in the accepted account application (you). It governs your self-directed forex and CFD account. Any separate service requires its own terms. We act as [principal and counterparty, or agent with identified counterparty]. Saint Lucia incorporation of Varexa Markets Ltd does not confer a forex or CFD brokerage licence.

Account opening and authority

You must meet the eligibility criteria for your place of residence and client classification and provide accurate information. Tell us promptly if your details, beneficial ownership, tax residency or authority to act change. Use the account only for yourself or through a representative we have approved. Keep credentials confidential and notify [account security contact] promptly of suspected unauthorised access. We will investigate reported activity and assess responsibility under applicable law.

Products and applicable documents

Before accepting, review the Risk Disclosure, Order Execution Policy, Trading Conditions and Client Funds and Payments schedule. This Agreement governs your account. Product specifications and fee schedules apply to the instruments and account type specified. Mandatory rights under applicable law prevail. A CFD gives exposure to price movements without ownership of the underlying asset.

Orders and confirmations

Submit instructions through [approved channels]. An instruction may be rejected for insufficient margin, market closure, an invalid order or a legal restriction. A submitted order is not a confirmed fill. Check prices, quantities and execution reports and notify [trade queries contact] promptly of discrepancies. We will review disputed fills using order logs and available market data. Corrections for objectively verifiable pricing or recording errors will be explained to you, subject to legal restrictions and your right to dispute the correction.

Charges and currency conversion

Applicable spreads, commissions, financing, conversion and account fees appear in Trading Conditions [URL or dated schedule]. Charges may be included in the quoted price or separately posted to your account. Conversion uses [rate source, calculation time and markup]. We will communicate changes under the notice provisions below.

Margin and close-out

Maintain the margin required for your positions. Account margin level is calculated as [approved formula]. Initial and ongoing margin requirements and stop-out thresholds are in [account schedule]. We may close positions under the disclosed rules when required margin is not maintained. A margin alert may not be received or provide time to deposit funds. Positions are closed in [sequence or method], subject to available prices. [State negative balance treatment, limits, eligibility and whether a deficit may be payable.]

Client funds and payments

Deposits are received by [legal entity and role] and held by [holding entity and account structure]. [State whether funds are segregated, how they are treated on insolvency, and whether any compensation scheme applies.] Deposit and withdrawal methods, fees, limits, processing times and rules for returning funds to their source appear in [dated payment schedule or URL]. We may request verification or pause a payment where justified by applicable law, a security concern or a mismatch in funding details. Where permitted, we will explain the status and next steps. A withdrawal may be limited to available funds after allowing for margin, fees and unsettled amounts.

Suspension and closure

You may request closure via [channel]. Outstanding positions and amounts must be addressed before the account can be settled. We may restrict or close an account for a material breach, fraud or security concerns, a legal requirement or loss of eligibility. Where practicable and legally permitted we will give notice, explain the reason and give you an opportunity to address it. We will state how open positions and undisputed balances are handled. Rights and obligations arising before closure survive where applicable.

Changes and notices

We send notices through [approved channels]. We will give [notice period] advance notice of material changes unless law or urgent circumstances require earlier action. We will explain the effective date and how to close the account if you disagree, subject to settlement of open positions and outstanding amounts.

Complaints and governing law

Send complaints to [email or address]. Include your account reference, relevant dates and the outcome you seek. We aim to acknowledge complaints within [period] and issue a substantive response within [period], or explain the delay. [State governing law, courts or approved dispute mechanism, and verified external escalation if available.] Mandatory consumer rights remain applicable.

Acceptance

Read this agreement and the linked schedules before accepting. We record the version accepted and the date of acceptance. A copy is available through [channel].
